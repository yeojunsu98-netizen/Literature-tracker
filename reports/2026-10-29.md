### 2. [Enhancing Interfacial Exciton Conversion in Blue Upconversion OLEDs through Oligomer-Controlled Molecular Orientation](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv.15011284/v1)

**Authors:** Hiroto Iwasaki, Naoya Aizawa, Kenichiro Omoto, Hayato Kawamoto, Daiki Nakahigashi, Yutaka Majima, Eri Sakuda, Ken-ichi Nakayama, Seiichiro Izawa  
**Date:** 2026-10-29  
**DOI:** `10.26434/chemrxiv.15011284/v1`  
**Source(s):** Crossref  
**Keyword score:** 12  
**Matched:** oled, organic light-emitting diode, blue oled  

**Abstract**

Controlling molecular orientation is central to high-performance organic optoelectronics. Anthracene oligomers provide an orientation-tunable platform for elucidating orientation effects in upconversion organic light-emitting diodes (UC-OLEDs), which generate blue emission at exceptionally low driving voltages through interfacial charge-transfer (CT) states, CT-to-triplet (CT-to-T1) conversion, and triplet–triplet annihilation. Across anthracene derivatives, increasing horizontal orientation enhances external quantum efficiency (EQE) beyond that expected from light outcoupling alone. To identify the underlying processes, the randomly oriented monomer 1,2-ADN and the preferentially horizontally oriented dimer p-ADN-1 serve as representatives. Both interface-exchanged devices, in which interfacial and emitting-layer anthracene species are varied independently, and transient absorption measurements consistently show that horizontal orientation enhances interfacial CT-to-T1 conversion. Overall, a more horizontal orientation of the host molecules improves light outcoupling by only ~4% but enhances interfacial CT-to-T1 conversion efficiency by 1.3–1.5-fold. Beyond its ultralow turn-on voltage of 1.5 V, the optimized p-ADN-1 device achieves an EQE of 2.61%, a 1.5-fold improvement over the 1,2-ADN device, and a LT50 (time to 50% of the initial luminance) exceeding 1750 h at 1000 cd m−2. To our knowledge, these values represent the highest EQE and longest operational lifetime reported for blue OLEDs with turn-on voltages below 2 V. These findings establish molecular orientation as an active structural parameter governing both light outcoupling and interfacial exciton conversion.

### 17. [Supramolecular Pulley Effect Enabling Highly Stretchable Transparent Electrodes](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsami.6c18620)

**Authors:** Xin Xu, Ning Mao, Hanyu Li, Mengqi Zhang, Jiawen Song, Jian Huang, Yufeng Song, Hongbin Liu, Xue Liu, Shuo Yang, Yang Liu, Bowen Cheng  
**Venue/category:** ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces  
**Date:** 2026-10-29  
**DOI:** `10.1021/acsami.6c18620`  
**Source(s):** Crossref  
**Keyword score:** 7  
**Matched:** stretchable  

**Abstract**

Abstract Silver nanowires (AgNWs) are promising materials for stretchable transparent electrodes (STEs) due to their high aspect ratio, excellent electrical conductivity, and optical transparency. However, the flexibility of AgNW-based STEs is often constrained by the significant modulus mismatch between the rigid AgNWs and soft polymer substrates. Herein, we introduce acrylated polyrotaxane (PRA) between the AgNW network and a polyurethane (PU) substrate, resulting in a highly stretchable and transparent electrode. The pulley effect of the acrylated polyrotaxane helps alleviate stress concentration at the AgNW–PU interface. Therefore, the network structure of AgNWs can remain relatively intact even under large tensile strains. The resulting composite electrode combines a transmittance of about 86.8%, a sheet resistance of 47 Ω·sq–1, and a stretchability of up to 130%. The as-prepared AgNWs/PRA/PU electrode demonstrates great potential for applications in stretchable electroluminescent devices and electrophysiological signal monitoring. This study offers a straightforward and effective strategy for developing high-performance stretchable electrodes, presenting a viable pathway for next-generation electronic devices.

### 19. [Advances in OLED Technology: From Fundamental Materials, Device Architectures to Emerging Applications](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv.15011255/v1)

**Authors:** Hina Goyal, Bhuvaneshwari S Baranwal, Leilani M Lotti Diaz, Robert Bird, Ankush Maind, Sudarshan Murmu, Umesh T Balande, Qiongqiong Angela Zhou  
**Date:** 2026-10-29  
**DOI:** `10.26434/chemrxiv.15011255/v1`  
**Source(s):** Crossref  
**Keyword score:** 7  
**Matched:** oled, organic light-emitting diode  

**Abstract**

Organic light-emitting diodes (OLEDs) have emerged as a leading optoelectronic platform, owing to their high efficiency, tunable emission, and mechanical flexibility, yet key questions remain about which molecular design strategies and device architectures are advancing commercial performance, and where persistent bottlenecks, such as blueemitter instability, continue to limit progress, Here, we address these questions through a data-driven analysis of approximately 86 thousand OLED-related documents from the CAS Content CollectionTM spanning 2016–2026, a period chosen to capture recent developments in this rapidly evolving field. This data-driven study provides a comprehensive assessment of advances across multiple dimensions of OLED technology. Global innovation trends reveal a pronounced concentration of intellectual property and technological development in East Asia, reflecting strong alignment between research activity and manufacturing infrastructure. Materials-level analysis highlights diverse molecular motifs governing key processes such as charge transport, exciton management, and emission. Concurrently, device architectures, including tandem structures, micro‑OLED platforms, flexible form factors, and hybrid integration, demonstrate a clear shift toward application-driven optimization. The application landscape indicates widespread commercialization in consumer electronics and display technologies, with expanding roles in lighting, automotive systems, wearable devices, medical technologies, and immersive platforms such as augmented and virtual reality. Despite ongoing challenges related to emitter stability, efficiency roll-off, and manufacturing complexity, emerging approaches in data-driven materials discovery and scalable fabrication are expected to enable further performance enhancements. Overall, OLEDs represent a mature yet continuously advancing platform with significant potential for next-generation display and integrated optoelectronic applications.
