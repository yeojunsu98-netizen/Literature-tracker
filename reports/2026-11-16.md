### 1. [Aggregation‐Activated Delayed Fluorescence in Phenothiazine‐Decorated Fluorenone: Interplay Between TADF and TTA](https://doi.org/10.1002/adom.72037)

**Venue/category:** Advanced Optical Materials

**Source(s):** Crossref  
**Keyword score:** 16  
**Matched:** thermally activated delayed fluorescence, tadf, oled  

**Abstract**

ABSTRACT We report thermally activated delayed fluorescence (TADF) that is activated by molecular aggregation, introducing the concept of aggregation‐activated TADF (AA‐TADF). In a donor‐acceptor molecule comprising a fluorenone acceptor and a tert‐butyl‐substituted phenothiazine donor, red TADF emission is selectively activated in the aggregated state. Photophysical investigations reveal two distinct emissive aggregated species, including a deep‐red‐emitting aggregate responsible for TADF, in addition to the monomeric emission. Oxygen quenching experiments and time‐resolved measurements demonstrate that delayed fluorescence is activated exclusively in this aggregated state. Fluence‐dependent measurements further reveal that the delayed emission contains a TADF contribution together with a triplet–triplet annihilation (TTA) component, whose relative weight depends on excitation density. This highlights that aggregation activates a triplet‐harvesting delayed‐emission manifold rather than a purely monomolecular TADF channel. Time‐dependent density functional theory (TD‐DFT) calculations suggest that aggregation induces a reorganization of the excited‐state manifold enabling AA‐TADF specifically within the axial conformation of the donor moiety in the aggregate. OLED devices reveal that the aggregation‐activated delayed emission channel is also retained under electroluminescence conditions. This study highlights aggregation control as a powerful post‐synthetic strategy for activating TADF and tuning emission properties, providing a new conceptual framework for the design of functional organic emitters.
