### 2. [Flexible 2D Donor‐Acceptor MOFs Enable Tunable and Pressure‐Responsive TSCT–TADF for Heterocrystal Photonic Logic](https://doi.org/10.1002/adma.75759)

**Authors:** Rui Sun, Lin Geng, Mei‐Hui Yu, Wei Li, Ze Chang, Xian‐He Bu  
**Venue/category:** Advanced Materials  
**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**DOI:** `10.1002/adma.75759`  
**Source(s):** Crossref  
**Keyword score:** 13  
**Matched:** thermally activated delayed fluorescence, tadf  

**Abstract**

ABSTRACT Efficient thermally activated delayed fluorescence (TADF) materials that combine high efficiency, color tunability, and dynamic stimulus responsiveness are highly desirable for adaptive photonic technologies, yet remain difficult to realize within a single crystalline platform. Here, flexible two‐dimensional (2D) donor‐acceptor (D–A) metal‐organic frameworks (MOFs) are developed through integrating an aggregation‐induced‐emission‐active triphenylamine donor with size‐matched electron‐deficient acceptors. Solvent‐directed assembly, donor conformational flexibility, and acceptor electronic modulation cooperatively regulate the D–A packing and through‐space charge‐transfer (TSCT) effect. The resulting MOFs exhibit broadly tunable TADF emission (474–635 nm), delayed lifetimes (0.11–7.65 µs), and photoluminescence quantum yields (PLQY, 0.52%–74.24%). Relative to the DMF‐derived structures, the DMA‐derived MOFs display increases of up to 51‐fold in delayed lifetime and 6‐fold in PLQY, while calculations support an unconventional high‐lying reverse intersystem crossing (hRISC) pathway. More importantly, the structural adaptability of the layered frameworks enables pressure‐induced reconfiguration of D–A interactions, giving rise to reversible piezochromic TSCT–TADF with emission shifts of up to 113 nm. A multicomponent heterocrystal is further constructed to translate pressure‐dependent emission changes into optical logic operations. These results establish flexible 2D D–A MOFs as a dynamic platform for integrating efficient TADF, mechanical responsiveness, and heterocrystal photonic functionality.
