### 1. [In-chip volumetric printing of perfusable and stretchable collagen-I scaffolds for mammary tissue models](https://doi.org/10.1088/1758-5090/aebbf5)

**Authors:** Amelia Hasenauer, Kristian Ivkovic, Samuel Thalmann, Bin Wang, Marcy Zenobi-Wong  
**Venue/category:** Biofabrication  
**Date:** 2026-11-18  
**DOI:** `10.1088/1758-5090/aebbf5`  
**Source(s):** Crossref  
**Keyword score:** 19  
**Matched:** stretchable, crosslink, photocrosslink  

**Abstract**

Abstract Engineered epithelial models require three-dimensional extracellular matrix environments that support organized cell growth and allow independent access to luminal and basal compartments. However, many organ-on-chip (OoC) fabrication strategies rely on planar geometries, non-native materials, or multi-step assembly workflows that limit architectural complexity and experimental control. Here, we report a direct in-chip volumetric printing (VP) strategy for fabricating stretchable and perfusable collagen-I scaffolds inside custom OoC devices. A vitamin C-regulated ruthenium/sodium persulfate photocrosslinking system enabled high-fidelity printing of collagen-I into open-lumen architectures with ductal- and alveolar-inspired features. By generating scaffolds directly within the final culture device, this workflow eliminates post-print transfer and integrates defined collagen architectures with compartmentalized fluidic access and a mechanically actuable chip format. To support chip-based culture, printed collagen constructs were stabilized after fabrication using 1-Ethyl-3-(3-dimethylaminopropyl)carbodiimide/N-hydroxysuccinimide (EDC/NHS) (EDC/NHS) chemistry, which limited thermally induced collagen densification, improved shape retention, and maintained scaffold anchorage during perfusion. The chip design provided separate access to the printed lumen and surrounding basal compartment, which enabled compartment-specific fluid handling while preserving scaffold integrity during inflation, stretching, and perfusion of the printed construct. On the collagen-I scaffolds, human milk-derived mammary epithelial cells formed epithelial layers with tight junctions and lactation associated markers. The platform further supported perfusion culture, in situ staining, and whole-chip volumetric imaging. Together, this work establishes direct in-chip collagen-I VP as a biofabrication strategy for creating perfusable epithelial tissue chips with native matrix architecture and compartment-specific fluidic access.

### 3. [π‐Expanded Arylamine Ancillary Substituents on Silicon‐Bridged Multi‐Resonance TADF Emitters Enable Superior Horizontal orientation and Long‐Lifetime Blue Organic Light‐Emitting Devices](https://doi.org/10.1002/smll.76827)

**Abstract**

ABSTRACT Boron‐based multi‐resonance thermally activated delayed fluorescence (MR‐TADF) emitters are promising for high color purity organic light‐emitting devices (OLEDs), but achieving both high external quantum efficiency (EQE) > 20% and long operational lifetime remains difficult, particularly for pure‐blue emission with CIE y DPSi‐4BP‐BN and DPSi‐3DBT‐BN , by extending the conjugation of the peripheral arylamine units with biphenyl and dibenzothiophene groups, respectively. ### 4. [One Acceptor Core, Two Bright Pathways: CF 3 ‐Substituted Triazines Achieve Highly Efficient Intermolecular Exciplexes and Intramolecular TADF](https://doi.org/10.1002/ange.5044613)

**Authors:** Toshiharu Ide, Youhei Chitose, Ryota Zenke, Tse‐Wei Chen, Hsin‐Yi Wei, Zhe‐Wei Fan, Che‐Liang Lin, Gomathi Vinayakam Mageswari, Ja‐Hon Lin, Tzu‐Chau Lin, Chihaya Adachi  
**Venue/category:** Angewandte Chemie  
**Date:** 2026-11-18  
**DOI:** `10.1002/ange.5044613`  
**Source(s):** Crossref  
**Keyword score:** 16  
**Matched:** thermally activated delayed fluorescence, tadf, oled  

**Abstract**

ABSTRACT Intermolecular charge‐transfer (CT)‐based exciplexes provide broad design flexibility through independent donor and acceptor selection, but often suffer from low photoluminescence quantum yields (PLQYs) due to limited orbital overlap. In contrast, intramolecular CT‐based thermally activated delayed fluorescence (TADF) systems can achieve high PLQYs through controlled donor‐acceptor interactions, but generally require precise molecular design and substituent tuning. Here, we show that a CF 3 ‐substituted triazine scaffold ( T4 ) serves as a unified molecular platform for achieving highly efficient emission through both intermolecular and intramolecular CT pathways. The CzCF 3 ‐substituted derivatives T4‐2CzCF 3 and T4‐3CzCF 3 formed highly emissive intermolecular exciplexes with mCBP, achieving PLQYs of 100%. Incorporation of stronger carbazole donors afforded T4‐3Cz , which exhibited intramolecular CT‐based TADF with PLQYs reaching 100% in solution and 100% in an mCBP‐doped film. T4‐3Cz also displayed near‐infrared multiphoton absorption, with a two‐photon absorption cross‐section (σ 2 ) of 80 GM at 780 nm and a three‐photon absorption cross‐section (σ 3 ) of 2.94 × 10 −80 cm 6 s 2 at 1210 nm. Furthermore, OLEDs based on the T4‐3CzCF 3 intermolecular exciplex and the intramolecular CT‐based TADF emitter T4‐3Cz exhibited efficient electroluminescent performance. These findings establish the T4 scaffold as a versatile molecular framework that integrates highly efficient intermolecular exciplex TADF, intramolecular CT‐based TADF, multiphoton absorption, and OLED functionality.

### 5. [Donor–Acceptor Integration Modulates Reverse Intersystem Crossing in Multiresonant TADF Emitters](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpclett.6c03266)

**Authors:** Pratiksha Dad, Kittimeth Thammanatpong, Mahni Fatahi, Peter M W Gill, Eli Zysman-Colman, Girish Lakhwani  
**Venue/category:** The Journal of Physical Chemistry Letters  
**Date:** 2026-11-18  
**DOI:** `10.1021/acs.jpclett.6c03266`  
**Source(s):** Crossref  
**Keyword score:** 13  
**Matched:** tadf, oled, organic light-emitting diode  

**Abstract**

Abstract Multiresonant thermally activated delayed fluorescent (MR-TADF) compounds are attractive as emitters in organic light-emitting diodes (OLEDs) owing to their high photoluminescence quantum yields and narrowband emission. However, these emitters generally have moderate reverse intersystem crossing rate constants (kRISC), which causes triplet exciton accumulation and leads to, among other excitonic processes, triplet–triplet annihilation. This process results in significant efficiency roll-off in OLEDs at high luminance. Here, we report the photophysical properties of a boron–nitrogen doped MR-TADF emitter, tCzBN, and two of its derivatives, SpAc-tCzBN and TRZ-SpAc-DBN, using time-resolved absorption and emission spectroscopy. Kinetic modeling of their photophysical behavior reveals that combining a donor–acceptor core with the MR-TADF moiety in TRZ-SpAc-DBN results in a higher kRISC than tCzBN and its donor-appended analog, SpAc-tCzBN, while preserving the narrowband emission of the MR-TADF unit. We attribute this enhancement to the lower effective adiabatic singlet–triplet energy gap (ΔEST) and stronger spin–orbit coupling in this molecular design.

### 6. [Conformationally Adaptive Through-Space Charge Transfer TADF Polymer Dots for Bioimaging and Photodynamic Therapy](https://doi.org/10.1021/cbe.6c00141)

**Authors:** Nae Aota, Xujun Qiu, Leonardo Evaristo de Sousa, Piotr de Silva, Satoshi Minakata, Zachary M. Hudson, Youhei Takeda  
**Venue/category:** Chem & Bio Engineering  
**Date:** 2026-11-18  
**DOI:** `10.1021/cbe.6c00141`  
**Source(s):** Crossref  
**Keyword score:** 13  
**Matched:** thermally activated delayed fluorescence, tadf  

**Abstract**

Abstract The rational design of materials that simultaneously enable bioimaging and photodynamic therapy remains challenging because of their incompatible design principles. Herein, we disclose the synthesis of thermally activated delayed fluorescence copolymers and the development of their polymer dots, which enable both bioimaging and photodynamic therapy without additional photosensitizing segments. The copolymers incorporate a conformationally adaptive donor−π–acceptor architecture (donor = phenoxazine, π = xanthene, and acceptor = dibenzo[a,j]phenazine) as the emissive core, providing efficient orange-to-red thermally activated delayed fluorescence emission via through-space charge transfer, while effectively suppressing severe aggregation-caused quenching, even at emitter loadings as high as 15 mol %. Photophysical studies reveal that the hydrophobic copolymer possesses an extremely narrow singlet–triplet energy gap, favoring efficient intersystem crossing and reverse intersystem crossing. The formulated polymer dots, prepared either with or without a biocompatible surfactant, retain bright orange delayed fluorescence in water and exhibit efficient cellular uptake with minimal cytotoxicity. Furthermore, the polymer dots enable lysosome-targeted cellular imaging and photodynamic therapy, exhibiting a singlet oxygen generation quantum yield of 66% under white-light irradiation. Collectively, these findings highlight a promising strategy for the development of through-space charge-transfer thermally activated delayed fluorescence-based polymer dots for multifunctional nanotheranostic applications.

### 7. [Silver Nanowire for Mechanically Durable Electroluminescent Devices and Stretchable Printed Circuits](https://doi.org/10.1002/cnma.70408)

**Venue/category:** ChemNanoMat  
**Date:** 2026-11-18  
**DOI:** `10.1002/cnma.70408`  
**Source(s):** Crossref  
**Keyword score:** 7  
**Matched:** stretchable  

**Abstract**

Silver nanowires (AgNWs) have emerged as promising building blocks for stretchable electronics owing to their high aspect ratio, excellent electrical conductivity, and intrinsic mechanical flexibility. Here, we report a multifunctional AgNW‐based platform that simultaneously enables stretchable electrodes for alternating‐current electroluminescent (ACEL) devices and highly deformable conductive circuits. In ACEL devices, AgNWs integrated with elastomeric substrates deliver stable electroluminescent performance under complex mechanical deformations, including bending, folding, and twisting, retaining 87% of initial luminance after 200 cycles and maintaining >97% luminance at 5% tensile strain. The prestrain‐assisted spray‐coating strategy is further developed to construct AgNWs–AgP/NW composite electrodes with a hierarchical, crack‐regulated architecture. The resulting electrodes exhibit markedly suppressed resistance variation, with only a 4.85‐fold increase at 80% tensile strain and minimal degradation under 1000 cycles of stretching and bending. This work establishes a generalizable strategy for engineering strain‐adaptive conductive networks, offering a scalable pathway toward robust, multifunctional soft electronic systems.

### 8. [Programmable Peel‐Transfer Sintering of Liquid Metal Particles for Monolithic Stretchable Electronics](https://doi.org/10.1002/adfm.79714)

**Authors:** Xuan Li, Jialu Wu, Jingyi Ma, Lingfeng Li, Shaowu Tang, Ziyang Liu, Hangbo Zhao  
**Venue/category:** Advanced Functional Materials  
**Date:** 2026-11-18  
**DOI:** `10.1002/adfm.79714`  
**Source(s):** Crossref  
**Keyword score:** 7  
**Matched:** stretchable  

**Abstract**

ABSTRACT Fully stretchable electronics require conductors, dielectrics, and thermally conductive layers that deform together without mechanical mismatch. Liquid metal particles (LMPs) are attractive building blocks for such systems, but their gallium oxide shells leave LMP films electrically insulating, so controlled sintering is required. This work reports a peel‐transfer strategy in which LMPs sinter during a single peeling step, and establishes how processing parameters control the outcome. Particle diameter, peeling angle, peeling speed, polymer type, and substrate surface state govern the wetting‐driven capillary infiltration of the polymer and the position and stability of the fracture path, which together decide whether particles remain isolated or rupture and coalesce. These relations provide design rules for producing insulating, weakly conductive, and highly conductive patterns from one LMP ink. Applying the rules spatially, through particle‐size patterning, dynamic control of the peeling angle, and substrate pre‐patterning, makes the sintering programmable, defining conductive and insulating regions within a single LMP film at a resolution of 27 µm. Interdigitated electrodes with high‐permittivity dielectric regions and light‐emitting diode patches with integrated heat‐spreading layers, each fabricated in one peeling step, demonstrate the applicability of this strategy. These design principles offer a route to monolithic, mechanically matched stretchable liquid metal‐based electronics.

### 9. [Development of Lightweight UHMWPE Composite Panels by Positioning Stretchable Interlayers and Tailoring Fiber Orientation for Improved Low‐Velocity Impact Performance](https://doi.org/10.1002/adem.71395)

**Authors:** Rinku Pramanick, Bhupendra Singh Butola, Abhijit Majumdar  
**Venue/category:** Advanced Engineering Materials  
**Date:** 2026-11-18  
**DOI:** `10.1002/adem.71395`  
**Source(s):** Crossref  
**Keyword score:** 7  
**Matched:** stretchable  

**Abstract**

Development of lightweight composites capable of mitigating low‐velocity impact‐induced blunt trauma is critically important for improved protection. The impact response of multilayered ultra‐high molecular weight polyethylene (UHMWPE) laminates has been investigated by many researchers in the last two decades. This study presents a development where thermoplastic polyurethane (TPU) film as an interlayer is strategically incorporated in UHMWPE composite panels to reduce near mid‐plane shear‐induced damage and back‐face deformation (BFD). All composite panels are fabricated via compression molding and tested at 100, 250 and 350 J energy using a low‐velocity impact tester. Viscoelastic behaviour is evaluated using dynamic mechanical analysis (DMA), and surface morphology is examined by scanning electron microscopy (SEM). Initially, an optimal sequence of cross‐ply with ±22.5° fiber‐oriented [CP/±22.5°] laminate shows the highest BFD reduction (60.48%) compared to ±30° (41.17%) and ±45° (34.77%). Moreover, symmetric placement of two TPU films near the mid‐plane of this [CP/±22.5°] panel doubles the impact load transferability and further reduces BFD by ~22%. Here, the intrinsic nature of TPU film improves viscoelastic energy dissipation and interlaminar shear management, while [CP/±22.5°] architecture governs the global laminate stiffness. The present approach exhibits that a combination of optimized fabrication, structural composition, and fiber orientation significantly reduces BFD in lightweight UHMWPE composite systems.
